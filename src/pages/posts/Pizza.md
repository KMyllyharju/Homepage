**Cooking time: 1h ish (Plus cold ferment at least over night)**

## Preamble

Adapted from the recipe of [@ajeatworld on instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ajeatworld?igsi=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ%3D%3D), altered to work well without a pizza steel and with the addition of a great marinara sauce (Also altered) from [Saveur](https://www.saveur.com/article/Recipes/Angelos-Marinara-Sauce/)

### Notes

For optimal results, allow dough to cold ferment for 2 days, but **at minimum** allow the dough to sit in the fridge overnight.

**You need to use pizza flour.** I tested the recipe with regular wheat flour, it just does not work the same. You need pizza flour to get a crispy bottom and a thin base.

## Actual recipe

Makes 3 pizza pies.

## Ingredients

- 500g pizza flour
- 320g **cold** water (32%)
- A packet of yeast (0.175%)
- 12.5g salt (2.5%)
- 10 ml oil (2%)
- 5g sugar (1%)

## Method

**If kneading by hand**

1. Mix ingredients in bowl.
2. Knead for 8-10 mins. Avoid adding flour. If too sticky let it sit for a few mins before kneading.
3. Rest under a bowl. After 20 mins stretch and fold, then cover again. Do this twice, resting for a total of 1 h.
4. Rest for 1 more hour.
5. Cut into three parts, fold balls in on themselves to make a round ball, then place in an oiled, covered dish and place in the fridge for 48 hours.

**If kneading with a mixer**

1. Mix ingredients in a bowl.
2. Knead with mixer for 8-10 mins.
3. Rest under a bowl for 30 mins, then stretch and fold.
4. Rest for 1 more hours.
5. Cut into three parts, fold balls in on themselves to make a round ball, then place in an oiled, covered dish and place in the fridge for 48 hours.

**Cooking**

6. Take dough out of the fridge 2-3 hours before baking.
7. Pre-heat oven to 250c with convection on, keeping the baking tray in, so it heats up as well. When preheated, transfer the pizza with the baking sheet on to the hot tray, and bake for 12 mins.

- 1 tsp Sugar
- 1/2 tsp Dried oregano

- 1 tsp Dried parsley
